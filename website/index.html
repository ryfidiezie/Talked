A quiet floating launcher for Windows.

Talked stays out of the way until you press Alt+Space. It handles quick calculations, unit conversions, window snapping, clipboard history, and real-time voice with Gemini.

What it does

Fast calculations

Type expressions, unit conversions, or currency rates straight into the search box. No need to open a separate calculator app.

Action grid

Hit Tab to switch to a visual grid of common desktop actions: snap windows, grab screen pixel colors, or mute system audio.

Local clipboard history

Talked quietly saves your recent copies in the background. Type /clip to search through past snippets and paste them back.

Gemini live voice

Direct streaming voice connection over WebSockets. Talk naturally with low latency when your hands are busy.

Common commands

/clip Browse and search recent clipboard history
/ip Show local and network IP addresses
/sysinfo CPU architecture, memory, and OS version
/time Current local time with seconds
/snap left Snap active window to the left half
/snap right Snap active window to the right half
/vol mute Toggle master system audio mute
/lock Lock your Windows workstation
